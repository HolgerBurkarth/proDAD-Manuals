# Overview of the User Manuals for proDAD Products

**proDAD GmbH** is a German software company specializing in video effects and production software. Since its founding in 1990, the company has developed creative tools and solutions for video creators, filmmakers, and content producers.

Most proDAD products (with only a few exceptions) are available both as a **SAL version** (Stand-Alone – an independent application, primarily for Windows) and as a **plugin** for popular NLEs (Non-Linear Editing systems). Supported host applications include *Adobe Premiere Pro*, *Grass Valley EDIUS*, *MAGIX Video deluxe / Video Pro X*, *Boris FX VEGAS*, *Corel VideoStudio*, *DaVinci Resolve*, and others. This dual availability allows flexible integration into existing workflows and benefits both professional users and ambitious amateurs in digital media creation.

This page provides a central overview of all available product manuals in different languages. Use the links to open the corresponding manual directly.
